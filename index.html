Under the helmets
GPU compute drives movement, targeting, body contacts, projectiles and damage. Sprites are baked at startup, then drawn in batches. A three-sector defense campaign and a separate mass-battle sandbox share the same GPU simulation. Large bodies use their own grid level. Persistent blood is stamped into a fixed-size terrain layer, not kept as extra physics objects.
Roots & inspiration
Built on Tront's BIOMASS crowd experiments. Inspired by mass-horde defense games including Crown Siege and Sir, We Have an Orc Problem. Independent project, no affiliation and no assets from those games.
