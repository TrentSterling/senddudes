Under the helmets
GPU compute drives movement, targeting, body contacts, projectiles and damage. Sprites are baked at startup, then drawn in batches. This is a single-file army-battle prototype, not the later recruitment campaign.
Roots & inspiration
Built on Tront's BIOMASS crowd experiments. Inspired by mass-horde defense games including Crown Siege and Sir, We Have an Orc Problem. Independent project, no affiliation and no assets from those games.
Original procedural art and code developed with AI assistance. MIT license. No analytics, accounts, external game assets or runtime downloads. Settings stay in this browser when storage is available.
